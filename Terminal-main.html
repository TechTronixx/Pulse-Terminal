        How Market Cipher B works
        • Fast wave (light blue) and Slow wave (blue): WaveTrend momentum. Fast above slow = momentum turning up.
        • VWAP (yellow): the gap between fast and slow. Above zero = fast is leading upward. Not the price VWAP.
        • MFI (green / red area and the band at the bottom): money flow. Green = buying pressure, red = selling pressure.
        • RSI (pink) and Stoch (green or red): stochastic lines. Stoch is green when it is below the pink line.
        • Levels: 60 / -60 (solid) mark overbought / oversold, 53 / -53 (dotted) are early triggers, 100 marks the top.
        • Small green dot: fast crosses above slow. Small red dot: fast crosses below slow.
        • Big green dot at the bottom: a cross up while fast is below -60 (oversold buy signal). Big red dot at the top: a cross down while fast is above 72 (overbought sell signal).
